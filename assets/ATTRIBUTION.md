# Third-party asset attribution

All 3D assets bundled under `assets/processed/` are dedicated to the public domain
under **Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0)**. There is no CC BY, no
ShareAlike, no NonCommercial and no NoDerivatives content in this set.

Under CC0 attribution is **not legally required**. We credit these authors anyway,
because both of them fund their work through it.

Full licence text: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
Legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>

Machine-readable equivalent of this file: [`ASSET_MANIFEST.json`](./ASSET_MANIFEST.json).
What was considered and turned down: [`INTAKE_REPORT.md`](./INTAKE_REPORT.md).
